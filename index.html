Explore the UK pharmacy map

The pharmacy directory includes names and addresses. Browse its map to find locations. The medicine file contains Egypt prices and no pharmacy links, so those prices can’t be assigned to UK pharmacies.

About this data  This CSV contains 4,930 medicine listings originally priced in Egyptian pounds and dated 19 Nov 2024. Displayed prices are converted at 1 EGP = ₸9.33 using the National Bank of Kazakhstan official rate checked Nov 16, 2026. Pharmacy inventory shown in profiles is invented demo data, not real stock or prices.
